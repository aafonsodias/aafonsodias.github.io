The extension reads every bookmark inside this folder, including subfolders. Name match ignores case. If no folder with this name exists, one is created in your bookmarks bar.
The regular schedule. On top of it, each series is checked two hours before its expected release and then hourly until the chapter appears, so you rarely wait more than an hour after a drop.
Only bookmarks inside the folder above are touched, and only when the series and chapter could be read from the title.
Recommendations come from the same databases. Skip hides one for 30 days; Not for me hides it for good.
Both are public, keyless APIs. If one stops working, switch the order.
