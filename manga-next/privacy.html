Manga Next privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

What the extension does

Manga Next reads the bookmarks inside one folder you choose (named Manga by default) and shows, for each series, the latest chapter available and when the next one is expected.

Data it reads

  • Bookmarks in the chosen folder: their titles and URLs, to work out the series name and the chapter you are on. No other bookmarks are read.

Data it sends

To look up release dates, the extension sends the series title (for example "Dungeon Odyssey") to two public, keyless services: Comick and MangaUpdates. Nothing else is sent: not your bookmark URLs, not your browsing history, not any identifier for you.

Data it stores

Results, settings, and the bin of removed bookmarks are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's extension storage. Settings may sync between your own Chrome profiles through Chrome Sync. Nothing is stored on any server operated by the developer.

Data it changes

If no folder with the chosen name exists, the extension looks for a folder that already holds chapter bookmarks under another name and offers to use it; only when none fits does it create an empty folder in your bookmarks bar. If the auto-cleanup setting is on (default), when you bookmark a newer chapter of a series the older bookmark of that series in the folder is deleted. Deleted bookmarks are kept in the extension's bin for 60 days so they can be restored. The extension never changes bookmarks outside the chosen folder.

Tracking and analytics

None. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts.
